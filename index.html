About Me

I'm Hok Chheangly, a UX/UI Designer passionate about creating simple, intuitive, and user-centered digital experiences.

With a background in Computer Science, I combine technical understanding with design thinking to bridge the gap between users, business goals, and technology.

I enjoy turning complex problems into clear interfaces and continuously improving my designs through feedback, iteration, and collaboration.

My Design Process

  1. 01Understand
  2. 02Explore
  3. 03Design
  4. 04Validate
  5. 05Deliver
Understand

Start with the problem

I begin by understanding the users, their needs, the business goals, and the context behind the problem.

Research • Goals • Users • Context

Explore

Turn insights into possibilities.

I explore different ideas, define the information structure, and map out user flows to find a clear direction.

IA • User Flow • Ideas • Information

Design

Shape ideas into experiences.

I transform concepts into wireframes and visual interfaces, focusing on clarity, usability, and consistency.

Wireframe • Design System • UI Design • Prototype

Validate

Learn from feedback.

I test and review the experience, gather feedback, identify problems, and refine the design based on what I learn.

Testing • Feedback • Iterate • Improve

Deliver

Bring the experience to life.

I prepare the final designs, document key decisions, and collaborate with developers to ensure the intended experience is carried through.

Handoff • Documentation • Collaboration • Development
